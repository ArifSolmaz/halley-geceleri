# Kıyı — audiovisual direction study

A 36-second local scene for Halley Geceleri. This is an unpublished visual and sound direction study, not a replacement for the complete film or a historical measurement.

## Image

`assets/reconstruction/halic-blue-hour.png` is an AI-generated artistic reconstruction, made with the built-in image-generation tool on 17 October 2026. The period reference was `assets/photo/halic-aksam-1913.jpg`, Auguste Léon / Archives de la Planète, 1913. The new image is not an archive photograph. Architecture, ships, costume and geography are interpretive and have not been independently validated. No astronomical objects were painted into it.

The runtime presents the image with a small camera approach, open-water displacement and limited funnel-smoke animation. It remains a moving illustration, not generated live-action footage. Reduced-motion preference disables movement. The mobile composition follows the boatman rather than cropping the middle of the city.

## Recordings

All three are CC0. Public HQ MP3 previews were downloaded from URLs exposed by their source pages. Originals and source-page snapshots are retained in the task's working folder, not bundled as purported historical documents.

- Félix Blume, “Seagulls on the top of Princes Island (Turkey)”, June 2012, published 11 October 2012. Adalar, Istanbul. https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/167129/
- Félix Blume, “Boat Horn”, June 2012, published 3 August 2012. Istanbul; the recordist describes a cruiser horn. This is a contemporary atmospheric element, not a verified 1910 steamship whistle. https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/162350/
- SpliceSound, “Paddle boat on water.wav”, published 12 May 2013. Recording location unspecified; not described as an Istanbul recording. https://freesound.org/people/SpliceSound/sounds/188199/

`assets/audio/quay-study.m4a` combines water at source 20–56s, gulls at source 10–46s, and one horn excerpt at source 0–6.8s delayed until scene time 15.5s. Water and birds have separate level and frequency shaping. The horn is filtered and given short low-level echoes to suggest distance. Final mix target is −20 LUFS, true peak no higher than −2 dBTP. No generated wind or noise bed was added. The levels, timing and playback were checked mechanically; an audible creative review remains necessary.
